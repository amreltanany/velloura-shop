What kind of bread does VÈLÔURÁ bake?

We bake a selection of sourdough, whole grain breads, focaccia, and classic artisan loaves, all made fresh in our kitchen.

Can I order VÈLÔURÁ bakes for takeaway?

Yes. Everything on the menu is available to take away, packed fresh at the counter.

Does VÈLÔURÁ offer freshly baked pastries?

Every morning. Croissants, danishes and seasonal pastries come out of the oven before we open.

Can I pre-order bread or pastries?

Yes, pre-order the day before and collect it from either of our stores.

Where can I find a VÈLÔURÁ bakery?

We have stores in Montréal (428 Rue Saint-Paul Ouest) and Toronto (315 Queen Street West).
